About Us

Meet the team driving strategy, creativity, and execution behind Ophelius.

Our Story

Built in Budapest. Built for businesses that want to matter.

Ophelius was founded in 2025 with one clear belief: small and medium-sized businesses deserve the same level of strategic marketing power as the world's biggest brands.

With a team of exceptional individuals from finance, marketing and computer science, the businesses that win are the ones people remember, talk about, and return to.

Our role is to make that happen with precision — creating marketing that doesn't just look good, but drives awareness, increases footfall, and turns attention into measurable growth.

Every strategy we build is designed for Budapest's dynamic, multilingual, tourism-rich market — modern, intentional, and focused on real business impact.
