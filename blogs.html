What does a marketing agency in Budapest typically charge?

We are transparent about pricing with no hidden fees and no long-term lock-in contracts.

How long does it take to see results from social media marketing in Budapest?

Most of our Budapest clients begin seeing measurable increases in engagement and foot traffic within 4 to 8 weeks. Our client Bijou Pest saw their first viral post within the first month, reaching over 100,000 organic views. Do I need to spend money on ads to grow my Budapest business online?

No. Our entire approach is built around organic growth — meaning we grow your audience and revenue without paid advertising. Through smart content strategy, SEO, and Google Maps optimisation, our Budapest clients have achieved up to 50% revenue increases with zero ad spend. Paid ads can amplify results but are never a requirement.

What types of businesses does Ophelius work with in Budapest?

We specialise in food and beverage, hospitality, bars, restaurants, retail, and tourism-facing businesses across Budapest. We understand the unique dynamics of the Budapest market — including seasonal tourist traffic, the multilingual customer base, and the competitive F&B landscape across districts like the V., VI., VII., and VIII.

What is the difference between SEO and social media marketing for a Budapest business?

SEO helps your business appear when people search on Google — for example "best restaurant in Budapest" or "cocktail bar Budapest." Social media marketing builds brand awareness and community on platforms like Instagram and TikTok. Both work together: SEO captures demand that already exists, while social media creates new demand. Ophelius offers both as part of an integrated Budapest digital marketing strategy.

Does Ophelius work with businesses outside of Budapest?

Our primary focus is Budapest and the surrounding Hungarian market, but we do work with regional businesses across Hungary and international brands looking to establish a presence in Budapest. Our multilingual team — fluent in Hungarian, English, German, Turkish, and Russian — makes us well suited for businesses targeting diverse audiences.

How does Google Maps optimisation help my Budapest business?

Google Maps is often the first place potential customers discover local businesses. Appearing in the top 3 of the local pack for searches like "restaurant near me Budapest" or "best bar Pest can dramatically increase walk-in traffic, calls, and website visits — all without paying for ads. We optimise your Google Business Profile, manage reviews, and ensure your listing ranks for the searches that matter most to your business.
